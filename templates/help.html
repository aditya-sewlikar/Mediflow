{{ icon("help") }} Frequently asked questions

The workflows clinic teams ask about most.

How do I book an appointment? {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
Open Appointments, choose the patient, doctor, and date and time, then submit the form. The doctor's schedule is checked for clashes and the next token is assigned automatically unless you enter one manually.
What is the difference between scheduled, waiting, and in consultation? {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
Scheduled means the appointment is booked but the patient has not arrived. Waiting means the patient has checked in and is in line. In consultation means the doctor is currently seeing that patient. Completed and cancelled are the closed states.
How do I move a patient through the queue? {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
Use the action button on the appointment row or the live queue board: Check in, Start, then Complete. Only one patient can be in consultation with a doctor at a time; complete the current visit before starting the next.
Why is a token number already taken? {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
Tokens are unique per doctor, per day. If you enter a manual token that already exists for that doctor on that date, choose the next available number or leave the field empty to let MediFlow assign it.
How do I find a patient quickly? {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
Use the global search in the top bar, or open Patients and search by name, phone number, or patient ID. Search results link straight to the patient record.
How do I create a clinic account? {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
Open the sign-in page and choose Register a clinic. Enter your clinic name plus the administrator's name, email, username and password. Registration creates an isolated clinic and a clinic administrator in one transaction. Clinic administrators cannot see other clinics or platform administration; platform administrators can view all clinics and choose one to work in from Platform Clinics.
How do I review all appointments, including past ones? {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
Open the appointment ledger from the dashboard or sidebar. It lists every appointment from the database with sorting, patient and doctor filters, status and date filters, and pagination. Today's rows are highlighted.
What does the completion rate include? {{ icon("chevron-down") }}
Completion rate is completed appointments divided by all appointments for the selected day or insight range. Cancelled and still-open appointments count in the total, so the rate reflects the full schedule.
